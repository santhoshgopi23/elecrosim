Everything for a control-panel drawing set

One tool from the first wire to the final PDF. No separate CAD, simulator and spreadsheet.

Live simulation

Voltage, current, power and state for every component, updated as the circuit runs. Faults and warnings appear in the results panel.

CAD-style drawing

Line, circle, rectangle and dimension tools with object snap, ortho and a command line. Create your own blocks and reuse them.

PLC, HMI and drives

Program a PLC, build HMI process graphics, and configure programmable VFDs and remote I/O alongside the power circuit.

Power and metering

Multi-function meters with CT and PT ratios, voltage monitoring relays, UPS backup and motor models.

Instruments and valves

Transmitters, process switches, electric and pneumatic valves with solenoid panels and limit switches.

Submission-ready output

Plot A5–A2 sheets with your own title block, cross-references, wire numbers and a priced parts list as PDF or CSV.

From idea to drawing in four steps

The workflow is the same whether it is a single relay or a full panel.

  1. PlaceDrag components from the searchable library onto the sheet.
  2. WireConnect terminals. Links and numbering keep multi-sheet projects traceable.
  3. RunPress play and operate switches, buttons and setpoints while it simulates.
  4. PlotExport sheets and the parts list to PDF, or save the project file.

Your work stays with you

ElectroSim has no server. Projects live in a file you control or in your browser, and the app keeps working with no connection.

  • Installable. Add it to your desktop or home screen and it opens in its own window.
  • Offline. Everything is cached after the first visit.
  • Private. Nothing is uploaded; drawings and parts lists are generated locally.
  • Portable. Save and share projects as plain JSON files.
